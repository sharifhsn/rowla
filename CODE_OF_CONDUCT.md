# Community conduct

Rowla welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, skill levels, and tools. Human and AI-assisted contributions are welcome.

## Work together

- Treat people with respect. Critique the change or the evidence, rather than the person.
- Ask clear questions and help new contributors understand the project.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes when the evidence changes.
- Give credit for other people's work.
- Keep discussions relevant to Rowla.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Moderation

The maintainer, [@sharifhsn](https://github.com/sharifhsn), moderates this repository.
For misconduct in a public thread, use GitHub's report controls or open a short issue with a link to that thread.
Do not repeat abusive content or disclose private details in a report.

The maintainer can remove harmful content or restrict participation. The response depends on the severity and repetition of the conduct.
This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and Discussions in this project.
